Read from the top of the map down. The Northern Edition takes
Tuesday 25 November 2026 in latitude order — the high north
first, the far south last — in black and bright emerald, and
holds every one of its thirty-seven stories to the same test:
dated today, or labelled with the day it truly belongs to.
Nine regions file their reports; the edition prints them all.

The northern file leads. Brussels unveils new sanctions on
Russia — drawn as an arc of emerald points over the continent,
because a sanctions package is a line drawn around a map.
Poland follows: NATO is sending early-warning jets after
Russian missiles landed on Polish soil, and Chancellor Merz's
promise of complete support is printed in the same entry, not
held for a later edition. Then the page travels to its
southernmost entry: Fiji, where the government has declared a
nationwide disaster in an El Niño dry spell — cracked earth
under a hard sun, and a declaration that makes the drought
official.

Between the poles, the rest of the day files in. Iran says
three tankers were hit by mines at Hormuz — filed under
CLAIMED, the edition's word for a report it cannot yet stand
behind. Baghdad orders the Kurdish oilfields seized. A new
count puts Russian war dead past 700,000. Xiamen Airlines
orders fifty Boeing jets; Madrid come from behind to beat PSV
5–1; twelve coral species are brought back on the Great
Barrier Reef; storms close on Thanksgiving travel; Washington
targets seven firms over chip diversion. The PCE report due
today is entered with its space left open — no figure had
landed when the edition closed, and an empty space is more
honest than an invented number.

The dates are policed at both ends of the page. Nvidia's rise
is filed under MONDAY CLOSE, where it belongs. Four stories
are refused entry and named in the OUT OF SEASON box: a
Mercury mission dated the 23rd, climate talks from earlier in
November, a Liverpool result from an earlier round, and
launch hype for a game that does not come out today.

For the reader: the eight heaviest figures run along the top,
each tagged with its card. The map is pinned by card number.
The day column reads north to south, the way the edition
itself does. Region counts cover only stories actually filed.

Set two ways before it was set once: `compose_northern.py`
lays the page out with a paper masthead over a wide lead, and
again with a black band over three leads abreast; the duel is
kept with the build notes, and the first setting stands.
`main.py` moves the finished page to `/output/entry.png`
byte for byte.

Wires: RFE/RL, Al Jazeera, DW, Starlit Daily, Forbes.
Drawings original to this edition.
